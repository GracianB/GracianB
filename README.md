## Professional universe · making complexity useful

I bring **16+ years across customer-facing work, teams and operations**, including international SaaS and customer service environments. I am interested in the friction between what a business intends to do, what its customers experience and what its people can actually deliver.

My work connects customer relationships and enablement with process design, knowledge, operational data, automation and collaboration with technical and product teams.
