## Purpose

This repository is not another project showcase. It is the navigation layer that explains the common thread across the professional work:

**People → Operations → Data → Systems → AI**

A reviewer should be able to understand the profile quickly, verify public evidence, and choose the right level of depth without reading a full CV first.

## Narrative contract

The final hub must communicate:

1. Customer and operational problems come first.
2. Data is used as evidence, not decoration.
3. Automation and AI are applied where they improve the system.
4. Software work is evidence of the ability to build, not a claim of being a framework collector.
5. Professional experience, public systems and yoga remain distinct tracks with clear boundaries.
6. Public claims must be defensible in an interview.
7. The public language set is Spanish / English / Italian.
8. Legacy project-navigation references that no longer belong in the profile are excluded.
